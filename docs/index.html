This project's thesis is not the positive result: it is the method. A single PASS on a variance target, surrounded by an extensive corpus of failures pre-registered before running, says far more than an isolated PASS — because the threshold existed before the number, and the negative results are written up with the same care as the positive one.

The input data

The starting point is a single series: hourly BTC/USDT candles from 2019 to today, plus the perpetual funding rate. There is nothing exotic here — deliberately: the question this project answers is how far price and volume alone can take you, and answering it requires not cheating by adding sources that would explain the result in the model's place.

Two things are added to that base. A macro block of 90 daily series (rates, indices, the dollar, equity volatility), carried onto the hourly grid with a causal fill: every bar sees only the latest already-published figure at that moment, never the next one. And three options data streams collected forward — implied volatility surface, order book, trades — which no API retains retroactively: if you do not record them as they happen, they are gone. That is why a collector has been running around the clock for months.

The features

Nothing works on absolute prices: everything is expressed in log returns, which are stationary and symmetric — a +1% and a −1% have the same magnitude, which is not true of prices. From there, 104 features are built, split into two streams the model treats separately: 86 dynamic (changing every bar) and 18 structural (position in range, distance from levels, volume profile, funding).

15 features were removed, and the reason matters more than the number. They were not dropped for being useless: they were dropped for being impossible to compute in real time. They look at 90- or 365-day windows, or require a fractional differentiation that needs the whole history. A model using them would get a backtest result it could not reproduce in production — the most common form of self-deception in this field. They are: dist_ath_365d, dist_ath_90d, dist_atl_365d, dist_atl_90d, frac_diff_close, frac_diff_volume, momentum_7d, momentum_90d, price_pos_365d, price_pos_90d, vp_concentration_long, vp_poc_convergence, vp_poc_dist_long, vp_vah_dist_long, vp_val_dist_long.

What is predicted

The production line's target is the realized variance of the next 30 hours, on a log scale. Not direction: the magnitude of the move, regardless of sign.

That choice is not cosmetic, and it is probably the most useful result of the whole project. On this asset, what can be forecast out of sample are the even moments — variance, dispersion, magnitude. The odd moments — the sign of the return, the asymmetry between rises and falls, the jump component — turn out to be unpredictable not only for the neural network but for the classical econometric baselines too. In the signed semivariance test the reference econometric model did worse than a constant: when even the traditional method loses to "do nothing", the problem is not the model, it is that the information is not there.

The model works on windows of 120 bars and predicts in a normalized space, not in price units. Converting back is a mandatory and delicate step: omitting it was the costliest error in the project's history — it produced stop-losses and take-profits wrong by two orders of magnitude, and a spectacularly false backtest. It is now protected by a check that fails the program rather than letting implausible values through.

How it is decided whether something worked

This is the part the project regards as its main content. A forecasting engine is judged by its results; a method is judged by what would have happened had the result been different. The rules below exist to make that question verifiable rather than rhetorical, and nearly all of them were written after getting something wrong.

  1. The threshold is written before the number. Every experiment is pre-registered and committed before it runs: the question, the expected prior with its direction, the metrics, the thresholds and the minimum number of observations. The commit is dated, so the ordering between criterion and result is not a claim, it is something you can check. Moving the goalposts once the result is in becomes a visible operation.
  2. There are three outcomes, not two. Besides passed and missed there is no conclusion: the case where the measuring apparatus cannot answer the question on that sample. Keeping it separate from failure is what prevents publishing as a negative result something that is merely a defect of measurement.
  3. The positive control, mandatory. Before believing "there is no signal" you must show that on that same window the apparatus detects an effect we know is there — typically beating naive persistence, the zero-parameter forecast. If it does not, the outcome is not a failure of the signal: it is a statement about the window. On its first application this control turned an apparently publishable negative result into a "no conclusion", which was the correct reading.
  4. Count before measuring. Many of an experiment's conditions do not depend on the model — how many observations exist, how often a rule triggers, how many are actually observable. They can be computed before spending compute or months of sample, and they should be: two experiments were redesigned because that count showed in advance they would have returned "no conclusion" for want of sample.
  5. Missing data is never "no effect". A hole in the recording stays a hole and is counted separately. It is a boring rule that has already saved two conclusions: a collector blind at certain hours of the day would have made a rule look "never triggered" when it triggered precisely in those hours.
  6. Validate on validation; the test split is touched once. The test split is not a bench to iterate on: it is consumed once, after the criterion has been met on validation, and with the same definitions.
  7. Every experimental lever is born switched off. A change under test must not be able to alter production behaviour until someone switches it on explicitly, and the absence of effect while off is verified on real data, not assumed.
  8. The negative result gets written up anyway, with the same numbers and the same care as the positive one. A documented failure is what stops you from trying the same road again in six months having forgotten why it was abandoned.

These numbers are not a score. A single PASS answers the question «is there a signal»: the other 4 concern the yardstick itself. Passing a method or reproducibility gate does not add a result: it changes or certifies the comparison instrument. Where the instrument changed it got stricter — the reference econometric baseline was replaced by a stronger one, and the claimed edge shrank accordingly.

The discipline is enforced by this page, not merely declared. The generator that produces it refuses to build if a card belonging to a still-open gate contains any decisional quantity — verdict included. This is not a reminder: it is an error that stops the build. A number seen early cannot be unseen, and every empty box further down is the shape that rule takes when it is respected.

The same holds for numbers already out: where a judge's report exists on disk, the page re-reads it at every build rather than hosting a copy. This is not a consistency check between two copies — it is the absence of the second copy, which makes divergence impossible rather than detectable. The measure comes from a real case: two numerical claims in this project had gone false without anyone noticing, because they had been copied by hand.

Assumptions and limits, declared

  • The result is specific to the hourly resolution. Repeated at half-hourly bars the same method fails its own criterion. Not a detail: it means there is no general law, there is a result at one precise scale.
  • On direction there is no out-of-sample edge at all, at any timeframe tried. The directional line stays in the code as a negative control, not as a strategy.
  • The operational arm is paper trading on a testnet. No real capital, no real slippage, no market impact, and transaction costs are assumed from public fee schedules rather than measured on fills.
  • Measured accuracy ≠ profitability. This is the distinction the project holds more firmly than any other: the entry rule built on top of the signal failed its own pre-registered criterion, while the passive comparison strategy stays positive. The risk premium exists; this rule does not monetize it.
  • The evaluation criterion rewards the conditional mean, the model optimizes a median. The mismatch is known, was tested with a standard correction, and the correction did not work — so it is documented as open, not as solved. It applies to both sides of the comparison, so it does not favour the model.
  • The effective sample is far smaller than it looks. Forecast windows overlap, so observations are not independent: where the raw data counts thousands of points, the statistically useful sample is worth a few hundred. Every significance reported here is corrected for this.
